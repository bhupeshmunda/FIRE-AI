# Research – LaundryTrack

## 1. Problem Statement

Students face difficulties tracking their laundry because the laundry shop does not provide clear information about when their clothes will be ready for collection. The shop operates only on specific days and remains closed on certain days, making it difficult for students to know when they can collect their clothes. As a result, students frequently visit the counter to check the status of their laundry, often wasting time and effort. There is no proper system to notify students about the laundry status, expected return date, or shop availability. This creates inconvenience for students and laundry staff.

## 2. Existing Solutions and Their Limitations

### 1. Manual Register / Token-Based System

Laundry shops commonly maintain paper registers or provide tokens to students when they submit their clothes. Students have to visit the counter to ask whether their clothes are ready. This system does not provide real-time status updates, expected collection dates, or information about the shop's operating days.

**Limitations:**
- Students need to visit the counter repeatedly to check their laundry status.
- There is no automatic notification when clothes are ready.
- Students may visit the shop on a day when it is closed.
- It does not provide an easy way to estimate collection dates.

### 2. WhatsApp / Phone Call Communication

Students can contact laundry staff through WhatsApp or phone calls to enquire about their laundry status. However, this requires repeated communication, and staff may not always be available to respond. It also does not provide an organised way to track orders or display the shop's opening and closing schedule.

**Limitations:**
- Students need to repeatedly call or message the laundry staff.
- Staff may not respond immediately.
- There is no centralised platform to view laundry information.
- Students may not receive timely information about shop availability.

## 4. Proposed Solution – LaundryTrack

LaundryTrack is a simple student-focused laundry collection tracking application designed to reduce unnecessary visits to the laundry counter.

The application will help students:

1. Record their laundry token number and submission date.
2. Estimate the expected collection date using historical laundry data collected from students.
3. View the laundry shop's opening and closing days.
4. Get a recommended date to visit the counter for collection.
5. Keep track of their submitted laundry information in one place.

### Expected Benefits

- Reduces unnecessary visits to the laundry counter.
- Saves students' time and effort.
- Helps students plan their laundry collection.
- Provides better awareness of the laundry shop's operating schedule.
- Makes laundry tracking more convenient for students.

### Limitations of the Proposed Solution

Since the application will not be directly connected to the laundry shop's internal system, it cannot independently verify whether clothes are ready. The collection date will be an estimate based on historical data, and students may still need to confirm the actual status with the laundry staff.
