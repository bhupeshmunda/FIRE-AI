# Reflection

## 1. What I misunderstood initially

Initially, I thought predicting the laundry collection date would be a simple task of adding a fixed number of days to the submission date. While working on the project, I realised that the actual delay depends on the laundry counter's working pattern and previous delays. I also understood that the promised date and actual ready date are not always the same.

## 2. What I would remove from my project

I would remove unnecessary features or information that do not directly help students know when to collect their clothes. I would prefer to keep the application simple, easy to understand and focused on solving the actual laundry problem.

## 3. Where AI helped me

AI helped me understand errors in my code, improve the structure of the project and think about how to handle different types of input data. It also helped me understand concepts related to calculating delays and testing the application.

## 4. Where AI wasted my time

Sometimes AI suggested solutions that were more complicated than what my project actually needed. Some suggestions did not match my existing code, so I had to spend additional time checking and correcting them. I learned that I should understand and test every suggestion instead of blindly accepting it.

## 5. What I learned

This project taught me that building useful software is not only about writing code. Understanding the real problem, collecting reliable data and testing the solution are equally important.
